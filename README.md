## The entry

> Speed-reading the terms and conditions: the gold goes to the first athlete to reach the bottom and still have a soul.

117 characters, one line. The event sounds like a real broadcast — a race with a gold medal — and the twist lands on the last four words: reading the fine print costs you your soul.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`). Stdlib only, no network. `main.py` validates the entry against the bounty gate before and after writing it: 1–200 characters, not only white space, single line.
